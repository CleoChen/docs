## What Sendify helps you do

- **Adaptive delivery:** Adjust sending speed based on throttling and provider responses.
- **Audience management:** Organize contacts with tags and dynamic segments.
- **Actionable analytics:** Track delivery, opens, clicks, conversions, and unsubscribes.
- **Compliance support:** Use built-in unsubscribe management to support privacy requirements.
