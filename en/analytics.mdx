After a campaign starts, open its details to review delivery and engagement.

<Icon icon="arrow-right-to-bracket" iconType="solid" /> `Campaigns` → `View details`

## Metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Recipients | Selected contacts minus excluded contacts |
| Sent | Messages successfully submitted for delivery; messages blocked by quota or invalid addresses are excluded |
| Delivered | Messages accepted by the recipient's mail server |
| Delivery rate | Delivered ÷ sent × 100% |
| Opens | Messages with a recorded open event |
| Open rate | Recipients who opened ÷ messages sent × 100% |
| Clickers | Recipients who clicked a tracked link |
| Click rate | Recipients who clicked ÷ messages sent × 100% |

### Recipient details

Sent and delivered views include the recipient address, name, tags, and delivery status. Open and click views also include the event count and most recent event time.

## Bulk actions

- **Apply tags:** Categorize recipients by open, click, or unsubscribe behavior.
- **Export:** Export high-intent addresses for follow-up.
