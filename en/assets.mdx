---
title: "Use the asset library"
description: "Upload, manage, and reuse image assets in email"
---

The Sendify asset library stores content that you reuse in email. It currently supports images.

## Upload assets

Upload an image from your computer or a URL. Bulk uploads are not currently supported.

<Frame>![Choose an asset upload method](/assets-upload-menu.png)</Frame>

## Manage assets

- **Preview:** Open an image directly or select **View details** from its menu.
- **Copy URL:** Copy the hosted image URL for use in an email template.

<Frame>![Preview an asset and copy its URL](/assets-copy-url.png)</Frame>

## Automated review

Sendify scans uploaded assets. Images that violate content rules cannot be used; open **View details** to see the reason.

<Frame>![Asset-review status](/assets-review.png)</Frame>
