<Icon icon="arrow-right-to-bracket" iconType="solid" /> `Home`, or the `Campaigns` page

<Frame>![Open the campaign list](/send-task-entry.png)</Frame>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create email content">Select **Create email content** and choose an editor.<Frame>![Choose an email editor](/send-editor-choice.png)</Frame></Step>
  <Step title="Configure the campaign">
    Set the subject, sender name, sender address, and reply-to address. Select contacts by tag or segment, optionally schedule the send, and enable invalid-contact filtering if needed.
    <Frame>![Campaign configuration](/send-task-form.png)</Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and send">
    Select **Next**, review every setting, and then select **Confirm send**.
    <Frame><img src="/send-task-preview.png" alt="Preview and confirm the campaign" className="mx-auto" style={{ width:"69%" }} /></Frame>
    <Frame><img src="/send-task-success.png" alt="Campaign created successfully" className="mx-auto" style={{ width:"73%" }} /></Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>Before sending, carefully verify the audience, message content, sender address, and scheduled time.</Warning>
