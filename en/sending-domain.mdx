Verify a sending domain before sending email with Sendify. Domain verification protects your sender identity, improves deliverability, and reduces the risk of messages being treated as spam.

## Choose a sending mode

Sendify supports branded sending and platform relay.

| Capability | Branded sending | Platform relay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visible From address | Your brand, such as `name@yourcompany.com` | Your brand, such as `name@yourcompany.com` |
| Envelope sender (MailFrom) | Your domain | Sendify's platform domain |
| Brand independence | Highest | Lower because Sendify handles the underlying delivery identity |
| DNS setup | Usually SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and MX | Usually DKIM and DMARC |
| Best for | Teams that need independent reputation and brand alignment | Teams that prioritize faster setup and lower direct domain risk |

### Branded sending

All technical layers of the message use your domain. This gives you the clearest brand identity and an independent sending reputation.

<Warning>
  Poor list quality or a high complaint rate can damage your domain reputation and may affect corporate mail. Use a dedicated subdomain such as `service.yourcompany.com` whenever possible.
</Warning>

### Platform relay

Recipients still see your branded From address, while Sendify handles MailFrom and delivery.

<Frame>
  ![Platform relay notice](/platform-relay-warning.png)
</Frame>

Platform relay requires less DNS configuration, but its shared delivery reputation can be affected by other users. Some strict recipients may also place less trust in a message whose From and MailFrom domains do not align.

## Add a sending domain in Sendify

<Icon icon="arrow-right-to-bracket" iconType="solid" /> `System Management` → `Domain Management`

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the sending domain">
    Sign in as an administrator and select **Add sending domain**.

    <Frame>![Add a sending domain](/add-sending-domain.png)</Frame>

    To avoid conflicts with existing SPF and MX records, use a dedicated subdomain such as `mail.yourcompany.com` or `mkt.yourcompany.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sending mode">
    Select branded sending or platform relay based on the tradeoffs above.

    <Frame>![Choose a sending mode](/sending-mode-choice.png)</Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure DNS records">
    At your domain registrar or DNS provider, add or update the host names, record types, and values shown by Sendify.

    <Frame>![DNS records required by Sendify](/sending-dns-records.png)</Frame>

    <Warning>If an SPF record already exists, update it as instructed. Do not remove existing `include` entries that are required by other mail services.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for verification">
    DNS changes normally take a few minutes to several hours to propagate. Sendify checks the records automatically.

    <Frame>![Waiting for domain verification](/sending-domain-verifying.png)</Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## DNS record glossary

- **MX:** Identifies the servers that receive mail for your domain.
- **SPF:** Lists the servers allowed to send mail on behalf of your domain.
- **DKIM:** Adds a cryptographic signature that proves the message is authorized and unchanged.
- **DMARC:** Tells receiving providers how to handle messages that fail SPF or DKIM and can produce abuse reports.
- **TXT:** May be used by Sendify to verify that you control the domain.
