Recipients can unsubscribe when they no longer want your email. Sendify manages unsubscribe links, opt-out records, and subscription restoration to support compliance and sender reputation.

## Insert an unsubscribe link

Every marketing message should include a visible and working way to unsubscribe.

### Insert a link from the editor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the email editor">Open the content editor for your campaign.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the insertion point">Place the cursor where the link should appear.</Step>
  <Step title="Insert the unsubscribe link">Select **Insert unsubscribe link** and choose a language.</Step>
</Steps>

  <Tab title="Rich-text editor"><Frame>![Insert an unsubscribe link in the rich-text editor](/unsubscribe-editor-rich.png)</Frame></Tab>

</Tabs>

### Use custom linked text

Select your unsubscribe wording, insert a hyperlink, and use `${SYS:UNSUBSCRIBE_LINK}` as the URL.

## What happens after a contact unsubscribes

- The contact is marked **Unsubscribed**.
- Sendify records the campaign and sender address associated with the opt-out.
- Future campaigns using that sender address automatically exclude the contact.

## View unsubscribed contacts

<Icon icon="arrow-right-to-bracket" iconType="solid" /> `Contacts` → `Unsubscribed contacts`

The list includes the email address, unsubscribe time, sender address, and originating campaign.

## Restore a subscription

<Warning>Restore a subscription only after the person has explicitly agreed to receive email again.</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open unsubscribed contacts">Go to `Contacts → Unsubscribed contacts`.</Step>
  <Step title="Find the address">Search for the email address.</Step>
  <Step title="Remove the unsubscribe status">Select **Remove**.<Frame>![Remove unsubscribe status](/unsubscribe-remove.png)</Frame></Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">Confirm the removal so the contact can receive future campaigns.<Frame>![Confirm subscription restoration](/unsubscribe-confirm.png)</Frame></Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

### What if my message does not include an unsubscribe link?

Mailbox providers may classify it as spam, which can reduce deliverability or cause enforcement actions.

### Can I link to my own unsubscribe page?

Yes. Sendify's built-in link opens the standard unsubscribe page, but you may add your organization's own link as regular linked text.

### Does the standard unsubscribe page support multiple languages?

Yes. It uses the visitor's preferred browser language when a supported translation is available.
