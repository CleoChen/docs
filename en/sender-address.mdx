After your sending domain is verified, create sender addresses and assign them to members who need to send email.

## Create a sender address

<Icon icon="arrow-right-to-bracket" iconType="solid" /> `System Management` → `Sender Addresses`

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open sender-address management"><Frame>![Open sender addresses](/sender-address-entry.png)</Frame></Step>
  <Step title="Create a sender address">Select **New sender address**, enter the address, and optionally set a display name.<Frame>![Create a sender address](/create-sender-address.png)</Frame></Step>
  <Step title="Review the address">Confirm the form and verify the new address in the list.<Frame>![Sender-address list](/sender-address-list.png)</Frame></Step>
</Steps>

## Configure a reply-to address

A reply-to address receives responses to your campaign. It can differ from the sender address, such as `support@yourdomain.com`.

<Note>Configure a reply-to address for each sender address so your team can respond to customer feedback.</Note>

## Assign an address to members

After creating an address, assign it to one or more member accounts. See [Manage member accounts](/en/member-settings#assign-sender-addresses).
